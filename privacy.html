This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Edergawi Calendar (Package Name: com.majid.edergawi), developed and published by Majid Ramli.

Catch It is a utility application that allows users to convert text into QR codes and share generated QR codes when they choose to do so. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

Information Collection and Use

Catch It does not collect, store, process, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information.

Any text entered into the application is processed locally on your device solely for generating QR codes.

No user account is required, and no personal data is sent to the developer or any external server.

Data Sharing

Catch It does not automatically share any user information with third parties.

If you choose to use your device's sharing features to share a QR code or text generated by the application, only the information you intentionally select will be shared. Such actions are entirely controlled by the user.

Data Storage

This app does not operate backend servers and does not store user data remotely.

Any QR codes, images, screenshots, or text saved by the user remain on the user's device and are not accessible to the developer.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Tracking technologies
  • User profiling services
  • External data collection services

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or users of any age.

Security

Because this app does not collect or transmit user data, no personal information is stored on developer-controlled systems.
